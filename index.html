About Amudham Snacks – Annai Jayam

Amudham Snacks – Annai Jayam began in a home kitchen with time-honoured family recipes passed down through generations. Every murukku, thattai and sweet is handmade in small batches, just like Amma made it.

We use carefully selected ingredients, pure oil and traditional methods — no shortcuts, no artificial colours, no preservatives. Just honest, delicious snacks for your tea-time, festivals and celebrations.
